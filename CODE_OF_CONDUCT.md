# Code of Conduct

Open Museum CMS welcomes contributors from museums, archives, historical societies, community organisations, academic institutions, and technical backgrounds.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, recognise different institutional capacities, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of private information.

Collections work can involve contested histories, cultural sensitivity, repatriation, community authority, and unequal institutional power. Contributors should approach these subjects with professional care and should not use the project to dismiss the rights, knowledge, or concerns of source and descendant communities.

Serious conduct concerns may be sent privately to **klemencicpuglisevich@gmail.com**.
